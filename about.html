About

Our StoryStarts Loud

One Crate.Then EveryoneWanted One.

Baby Babay started with a single gift crate — put together for someone who loves music as much as we do, with a onesie, a rattle, and a few small things that nodded to what was always playing in that house. It was supposed to be a one-off. Instead, everyone who saw it asked where they could get one too.

So we built the shop we wished already existed: gift crates organized by genre — starting with old school hip hop, rock n' roll and country, with more on the way — plus every piece sold on its own for anyone who just wants the one thing.

Nothing here is picked to fill a shelf. It's picked the way we picked that first crate: for an actual kid, and the actual music their family loves.
