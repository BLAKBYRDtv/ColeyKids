Our Story

Coley Kids started with a single gift crate — put together for someone who loves music as much as we do, with a onesie, a rattle, and a few small things that nodded to what was always playing in that house. It was supposed to be a one-off. Instead, everyone who saw it asked where they could get one too.

So we built the shop we wished already existed: gift crates organized by genre — starting with old school hip hop, rock n' roll, and country, with more on the way — plus every piece available on its own for anyone who just wants the one thing. Nothing here is picked to fill a shelf. It's picked the way we picked that first crate: for an actual kid, and the actual music their family loves.

Curated, not mass-produced
We're not stocking twelve colorways of the same generic onesie. Every item earns its spot, and every crate is put together by hand — not auto-filled from a catalog.
Made for music lovers
This started as a love letter to the music we grew up on, and that's still the whole point. Each genre gets its own crate, built for the households where it's more than a playlist — it's part of the family.
Built to be given
Baby showers, first birthdays, or just because — every crate ships gift-ready. No extra trip to wrap something that already looks like a gift.
Questions before you order?
Have a question, or want a crate built around a genre we don't have yet? Email us at hello@coleykids.com — we read every message.
