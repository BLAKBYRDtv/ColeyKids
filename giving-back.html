Why We Started This

Every crate we sell exists because someone put music in a kid's hands early.

That's the whole premise of Coley Kids. Not every kid gets that. Some grow up without an instrument to touch, a program to join, or someone to show them a first chord. The Little Legends Fund is our way of putting a little of that access back — starting small, starting local, and growing with the shop.
